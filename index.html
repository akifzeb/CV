Research Profile

Inorganic materials chemist working on redox-active materials, currently principal investigator on a Horizon Europe MSCA fellowship. I design materials whose local electronic structure is specified before synthesis, so that the material controls which reaction occurs at an interface. The material families are metal-organic frameworks and MOF-derived carbons, transition metal oxides, sulphides, selenides and fluorides, graphene oxide supports, single-atom sites, and nanodiamonds. I apply this design approach to photocatalysis and water remediation, nanozymes, electrocatalysis and CO2 reduction, and electrochemical energy storage.
