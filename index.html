Career Objectives

I am a dedicated science professional with a passion for pioneering research and transformative teaching in the design and development of advanced functional materials. I have extensive interdisciplinary experience in the synthesis, design, development and characterization of advanced materials/nanomaterials through hydrothermal, colloidal and CVD methods and their applications in photocatalysis, photoelectrocatalysis, heterogeneous catalysis, electrocatalysis, green chemistry, organic synthesis HER, OER, CO2RR, EGOR, Li/Na-ion batteries, supercapacitor, sensing and biocatalysis, protein & enzyme mimics, environmental remediation and analytical chemistry and the catalytic mechanisms involved in heterogenous processes.
